WHO WE ARE

From clearing to final surface.

Established in May 2024, Etowah Construction Group delivers site development, underground utility, roadway, and pump-station construction for municipal, commercial, and residential clients.

Our completed work includes full-site packages, large-diameter water infrastructure, pump-station rehabilitation, DOT-road paving, deep excavation, bypass operations, and critical public-roadway tie-ins.
